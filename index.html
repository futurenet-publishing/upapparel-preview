Our story

More than clothing. A mindset.

UP Apparel was founded by British Army veteran James, inspired by 24 years of service and the values that stayed with him long after leaving: discipline, loyalty and purpose.

Our motto, Utrinque Paratus, is Latin for “ready for anything”. You don’t need a military background to share it. It’s about showing up, putting in the work and standing by what matters.

Questions

Good to know

All questions
How do UP Apparel T-shirts and hoodies fit?

All our T-shirts and hoodies are a unisex fit. Our heavyweight tees have a relaxed, structured shape that holds its fit wash after wash. Check the size guide for chest, length and sleeve measurements; most tees come in S to 4XL and hoodies in S to 3XL.

How long does delivery take?

Our clothing is made to order, so please allow 5–10 working days for production and UK delivery. Delivery costs are shown at checkout.

What are your T-shirts made from?

Our premium heavyweight T-shirts are 100% ring-spun cotton at 206.8 g/m², garment-dyed for a soft, lived-in feel, with reinforced stitching. They’re machine washable.

How does UP Apparel support veterans?

UP Apparel was founded by a British Army veteran, and every order helps support a veteran. Name the charity and the amount per order here.
