Behind the designs

The stories behind our special editions

Each UP Apparel special edition marks a story that matters to the forces community. Here’s what each design honours.

Arnhem

In September 1944, during Operation Market Garden, British airborne troops landed near the Dutch town of Arnhem to seize the bridge over the Rhine. Cut off and heavily outnumbered, they held the north end of the bridge for days before being overrun. The battle has become a lasting symbol of standing firm against overwhelming odds.

The Arnhem Special Edition carries a monochrome Arnhem bridge design on the back, finished with Utrinque Paratus and Ready for Anything, and the clean UP chest mark on the front.

Arnhem Special Edition Hoodie Arnhem Special Edition T-Shirt

St Michael

St Michael the Archangel is the patron saint of paratroopers, long associated with courage and protection. Our St Michael designs show the archangel standing victorious, sword raised and shield in hand, and, on the Demon edition, over the demon with paratroopers descending from the night sky.

They’re made for those with a connection to the airborne community, or anyone drawn to the strength and meaning behind the design.

St Michael Special Edition T-Shirt St Michael Demon T-Shirt St Michael Hoodie

St Christopher

St Christopher is the patron saint of travellers, carried for centuries as a symbol of protection on the road ahead. Our St Christopher edition carries his image on the back, standing for protection, resilience and the mindset to face whatever lies ahead.

St Christopher Special Edition T-Shirt St Christopher Hoodie

Remembrance

Every year on 11 November, and on Remembrance Sunday, the nation pauses to remember those who served and those who gave their lives. Our Remembrance Day edition carries the artwork “Lest We Forget – 11•11 – We Will Remember Them”, a battlefield scene with remembrance poppies.
