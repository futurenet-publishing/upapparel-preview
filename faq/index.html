Frequently asked questions

Sizing, delivery, returns and more. Can’t find the answer? Get in touch.

How do UP Apparel T-shirts and hoodies fit?

All our T-shirts and hoodies are a unisex fit. Our heavyweight tees have a relaxed, structured shape that holds its fit wash after wash. Check the size guide for chest, length and sleeve measurements; most tees come in S to 4XL and hoodies in S to 3XL.

How long does delivery take?

Our clothing is made to order, so please allow 5–10 working days for production and UK delivery. Delivery costs are shown at checkout.

What are your T-shirts made from?

Our premium heavyweight T-shirts are 100% ring-spun cotton at 206.8 g/m², garment-dyed for a soft, lived-in feel, with reinforced stitching. They’re machine washable.

How does UP Apparel support veterans?

UP Apparel was founded by a British Army veteran, and every order helps support a veteran. Name the charity and the amount per order here.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Returns window, how to start a return and who pays postage.

Will special editions come back?

Special editions are available for a limited time. Once they’re gone, they’re gone, so if a design means something to you, don’t wait. Sign up to our emails for early access to new editions.

Do you make women’s clothing?

Our women’s collection is coming soon, starting with leggings and essential tops. All our current T-shirts and hoodies are unisex.

What does Utrinque Paratus mean?

Utrinque Paratus is Latin for “ready for anything”. It’s the UP Apparel motto and appears on every garment.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. UP Apparel gift cards come in £10, £25, £50 and £100.
